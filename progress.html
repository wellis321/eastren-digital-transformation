Outcomes & Assets to Date

What the programme has established, produced, and put in place during the discovery and mobilisation phase.

Knowledge & Learning

Core concepts introduced to build modern, user-centred capability across the team:

  • Agile (incl. Agile Manifesto)
  • Accessibility / A11y / Web Standards
  • User Story Mapping
  • Gestalt principles for group work
  • Introduction to Databases and APIs
  • Schemas and data structures
  • Poka Yoke (mistake-proofing)
  • Data-Driven Design
  • Scottish Government Design System
  • Needs-led Design Principles

Tools & Platforms

Collaboration and planning infrastructure established for the team:

  • SharePoint (structured document hub — ENV/99/24)
  • Microsoft Planner (multiple planners by workstream)
  • Microsoft Lists (stakeholders, reading)
  • OneNote shared workspace
  • Whiteboards (discovery, risks, ideas, learning)
  • Loops
  • Visio
  • Teams channel for questions and updates

Research & Discovery Assets

Evidence gathered to inform future design and delivery decisions:

  • 18-month delivery plan (online)
  • Resource library (curated links)
  • Benchmarking of peer councils
  • User reporting pathway maps
  • API issues analysis
  • Links to tenant-facing tools and council systems
  • National and regional context overview
  • Sector comparisons and themes, risks, lessons learned

Planning & Governance

Structured artefacts to organise work and engage stakeholders:

  • Stakeholder mapping and introduction materials
  • Project delivery guidance
  • Project groups and structures defined
  • Occupational development resources
  • Self-development opportunity mapping
  • Change Agent Forum participation

Project-Specific Outputs

Outputs contributing directly to shaping future solutions:

  • Existing and new process maps (saved to SharePoint)
  • Community benefits documentation
  • NEC upgrade testing artefact
  • Suggestions for global digital improvements
  • R&D across systems: SOR, BARIS, DRS, NEC, APEX, ROCC
  • Mobile Phone Project requirements

Emerging Projects & Workstreams

Discovery activity has identified the following emerging workstreams to be scoped and planned:

  • Tenants — tenant communication and engagement
  • Integra — P2P, financial controls, system interfaces with other departments
  • Job Numbers — Schedule of Rates, job costing
  • Systems — mobile working, Apex, phones, standardised data, documentation, testing, asbestos, address details (primary key)
  • Stores — stock ordering, van stock, stock control, replenishment, audit checks
  • Communication — feedback loops across BST, Trades, HMT, Tenants, Customer Services, Finance, Procurement and all Housing teams
  • Governance — user groups, service improvements, KPI/KPA reporting, accessibility, Plan B
  • ROCC Discovery — appointment scheduling, interfaces, travel time, supplier portal, contract management, completion time recording
