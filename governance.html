Project Group

Strategic governance — direction, decisions, and corporate alignment.

Project Group

Strategic governance and corporate alignment

Every 4 weeks
First meeting: 22 July 2026
Purpose

The Project Group is the primary governance body for the programme, chaired by the Project Owner. It provides strategic direction, ensures the programme remains aligned with corporate priorities, and holds accountability for delivery outcomes. It receives escalated risks and decisions from the Steering Group and approves major changes to scope, budget, or timeline. Membership spans the full breadth of services involved — repairs, finance, HR, ICT, procurement, business improvement, information governance, and community benefits.

Key responsibilities
  • Provide strategic direction and hold accountability for programme outcomes
  • Approve significant changes to scope, budget, or delivery approach
  • Receive and resolve escalated risks, issues, and blockers
  • Ensure alignment with corporate strategies, compliance requirements, and ERC priorities
  • Support procurement decision-making and supplier engagement
  • Champion the programme across their respective service areas

Steering Group

Cross-functional delivery oversight — keeps the programme on track and workstreams coordinated.

Steering Group

Cross-functional delivery oversight

Every 4 weeks
First meeting: 14 July 2026
Purpose

The Steering Group is the cross-functional delivery body for the programme. It reviews progress against the delivery plan, manages risks and issues at a workstream level, and ensures coordination across all service areas — repairs, finance, stores, customer services, HR, systems, and governance. It is the first point of escalation before issues reach the Project Group.

Key responsibilities
  • Review programme progress and delivery plan status every four weeks
  • Identify, log, and manage risks and issues across all workstreams
  • Coordinate activity across service areas to prevent gaps and duplication
  • Surface decisions and blockers that require Project Group input
  • Ensure procurement activity, system integration, and change readiness stay aligned

Technical Group

Operational and technical readiness — the frontline voice in shaping the solution.

Technical Group

Operational readiness and technical requirements

Every 4 weeks
First meeting: 13 July 2026
Purpose

The Technical Group brings together the people who use the systems day-to-day — scheduling staff, stores, finance operations, ICT, and systems specialists. Its purpose is to ensure that technical and operational requirements are captured accurately, that solutions are practical and fit for purpose at a frontline level, and that the teams most affected by change are engaged early and prepared. This group gives operational expertise a direct seat at the programme table.

Key responsibilities
  • Review and validate technical and operational requirements
  • Assess solutions from a frontline usability and practicality perspective
  • Identify integration dependencies and system-level risks
  • Represent the day-to-day operational experience in design and testing decisions
  • Support change readiness and ensure teams are prepared before go-live
  • Escalate operational concerns to the Steering Group
