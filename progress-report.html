1 Executive Summary

The Housing Repairs Digital Transformation programme is progressing well through its Discovery & Mobilisation phase. Since commencing in April 2026, the programme team has mobilised, established its infrastructure, and engaged all key operational teams in structured discovery activity. Positive relationships have been built across Customer Services, Stores, the Business Support Team, and the Housing Maintenance Team — with all four teams actively participating and contributing their requirements.

A consistent theme has emerged across every team engaged: the need to reduce manual effort, eliminate spreadsheet dependency, connect data between systems, and make the right information visible to the right people at the right time. These themes will directly shape the requirements baseline, vendor evaluation, and co-design work in the next phase.

Programme governance is now established. Three boards — Technical, Project, and Programme — hold their first meetings in July 2026. Eight distinct workstreams have been identified through discovery and are being scoped ahead of Phase 2.

4 Stakeholder Engagement

All four operational teams have engaged positively. Key requirements captured from each are summarised below.

Customer Services — Repairs Active — ongoing

Reduce spreadsheet use • Image uploads with job comments • Inspection plans visible in system • Property attributes surfaced (factored status, asbestos, APEX data) • Ad hoc meetings and workshops agreed

Stores Active — multiple sessions

Orders pre-built and ready to collect • Orders auto-generated from job requirements • Auto-replenishment based on stock levels • Contract prices activated on schedule • Reduce duplication and manual data entry • Workshops agreed; shadowing day proposed

Business Support Team Active — multiple sessions

High volume of manual workflows to be replaced • Integra (billing & invoicing) integration is critical • Requirements are nuanced and detailed — captured in process maps (SharePoint ENV/99/24) • ROCC noted to handle Integra integration well

Housing Maintenance Team (HMT) Active — ongoing

Mobile working for tradesmen • Voids management (keen to develop specifically) • Schedule of Rates (SORs) • Van stock management • Reporting and performance visibility • Mobile phone rollout completed • Joint workshops with Stores and Trades planned
