1 Executive Summary

The Housing Repairs Digital Transformation programme is progressing well through its Discovery & Mobilisation phase. Since commencing in April 2026, the programme team has mobilised, established its infrastructure, and engaged all key operational teams in structured discovery activity. Positive relationships have been built across Customer Services, Stores, the Business Support Team, and the Housing Maintenance Team — with all four teams actively participating and contributing their requirements.

A consistent theme has emerged across every team engaged: the need to reduce manual effort, eliminate spreadsheet dependency, connect data between systems, and make the right information visible to the right people at the right time. These themes will directly shape the requirements baseline, vendor evaluation, and co-design work in the next phase.

Programme governance is now established. Three meeting groups — Technical, Project, and Steering — hold their first meetings in July 2026. Eight distinct workstreams have been identified through discovery and are being scoped ahead of Phase 2.

Reduce spreadsheet use • Image uploads with job comments • Inspection plans visible in system • Property attributes surfaced (factored status, asbestos, APEX data) • Ad hoc meetings and workshops agreed

9 Sector Context & Benchmarks

A summary of what comparable organisations have done — and the lessons most relevant to this programme. Full case studies and benchmark costs are on the programme portal.

Organisation What they did Lesson for ERC
Glasgow City Council MyGlasgow app — 40% digital channel shift for repairs Channel shift follows active marketing as much as product. Tenants need to be encouraged, not just informed.
Edinburgh City Council AI chatbot pilot for repairs triage via Salesforce Community-level outreach is essential. Without it, tenants default to phone even when a digital option exists.
North Lanarkshire Council Optitime mobile workforce — 15% first-time fix improvement The gain came from operatives seeing full property history on device. Data quality is a prerequisite for mobile workforce benefit.
Dundee City Council SAtSD co-design with a diverse tenant panel throughout Co-design from day one means no costly remediation post-launch — WCAG 2.1 AA achieved at go-live, satisfaction above sector benchmarks.
Aberdeenshire Council NEC Housing system replacement over 18 months (agile) Data migration took 3 months longer than planned. Needs its own workstream with buffer — not a side task.
South Lanarkshire Council Digital telecare transition with dedicated inclusion workstream Vulnerable tenant digital inclusion must be a funded workstream, not a help page — with third-sector partners from the start.
Wheatley Group MyWheatley portal — 60%+ digital, video triage, AI scheduling The gold standard for Scottish social housing digital. Sets the long-term vision: 24/7 self-service, real-time tracking, post-repair satisfaction surveys.

Published cost signals (Public Contracts Scotland / Find a Tender)

North Lanarkshire — Hosted Job Manager & Dynamic Scheduling (Optitime): ~£899k (G-Cloud, 2020)
Fife Council — Civica Cx housing system implementation support: £120k (2024)
Leicester City — Mobile working contract (ROCC-related): ~£691k (England, Dec 2025)
Hammersmith & Fulham — BARIS contract line item: ~£65k standalone (to 2027)
Figures are published procurement notices. Verify on source before use in business cases. → View full Peer Benchmarks table on the programme portal
Invest in tenant engagement Digital channels need active promotion. We need to build a communications campaign into the programme plan from Phase 1 — not as an afterthought.
Treat data migration as a workstream Every comparable programme underestimated data cleansing. Scope and resource it separately from the system implementation.
Co-design saves money downstream Dundee's tenant panel approach delivered accessibility at launch. ERC should recruit a tenant panel in Phase 2 and keep them involved throughout.
Data quality enables every other benefit Mobile workforce, first-time fix, and AI triage all depend on accurate property and repair history. Data quality work should start now, in parallel with discovery.
