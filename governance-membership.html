How to use this tool
  1. Scan the roster under each group heading — that is the quick “who’s on this list” view.
  2. Your mark — Yes / Maybe / No is your opinion only; one mark per person per group.
  3. Tallies — hover counts to see who marked what (names only).
  4. Wrong list? — open Place on another group on a row: Also on keeps them here; Move to switches them.
  5. New group — create a custom list below, then move people onto it.

What we expect

  • Assess new names when you see the banner.
  • Name and role only — no emails or sensitive personal data.
  • Final membership for meetings sits with the Project Manager / Project Owner.
