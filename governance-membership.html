Membership tool

Who sits where — scan each group’s name list first, then mark your opinion. One Yes / Maybe / No per person per group; tallies are shared.

How to use this tool
  1. Each person's row shows their name, role, and your voting buttons straight away. Open More on a row for types, other lists they're on, or to place them elsewhere.
  2. Filter by type — use the chips under Groups (Champion, Decider, Influencer, and so on) to show only people with those attributes. Use In groups to limit which lists are shown (e.g. Influencers on Steering only).
  3. Your mark (optional) — Yes / Maybe / No is your private opinion on a person for a group. It is not required when someone is moved.
  4. Reason — click Add reason under a mark to jot a note. Tick Share with others if you want colleagues to see it (and which mark you gave); leave it unticked to keep it private to you.
  5. Tallies — counts of Yes / Maybe / No are always shared; individual marks stay private unless you share a reason.
  6. Wrong list? — open a row's More, then Place on another group: Lisa / William (confirmers) change the live list; everyone else sends a suggestion that stays tentative until accepted.
  7. × Remove — confirmers remove from the live list; others suggest a removal (person stays until accepted).
  8. New group — confirmers create a custom list below, then place people onto it.
  9. Person types — create types under Person types; assign them on each person via their row's More. Built-in types can be renamed but not deleted.
  10. History — membership and person-type changes are saved automatically. Open History for past compositions; filter a snapshot by status or search a name; Export PDF on the selected card.
  11. Group status — confirmers can mark a list Active / stable or In development. That does not stop anyone else suggesting moves.

Please remember

  • When you use Add someone below, enter their name and job title only — do not put in email addresses or other personal details.
  • Most people make suggestions; Lisa and William confirm the live membership lists and group status.
  • Full guide to this tool — features, history, exports, privacy, permissions, and how to suggest improvements.
