Membership tool

Who sits where — scan each group’s name list first, then mark your opinion. One Yes / Maybe / No per person per group; tallies are shared.

How to use this tool

  1. Scan the roster under each group heading — that is the quick “who’s on this list” view.
  2. Your mark — Yes / Maybe / No is your opinion only; one mark per person per group.
  3. Private reason — optional note under your mark explaining why (only you see it).
  4. Tallies — counts of Yes / Maybe / No are shared; individual marks stay private.
  5. Wrong list? — open Place on another group: confirmers change the live list; others send a suggestion that stays tentative until accepted.
  6. × Remove — confirmers remove from the live list; others suggest a removal (person stays until accepted).
  7. New group — confirmers create a custom list below, then place people onto it.
  8. History — membership changes are saved automatically. Open History for past compositions; use Milestones or Show older when the timeline grows; filter a snapshot by status or search a name; Export PDF on the selected card.
  9. Group status — each list can be In development, Active / stable, Inactive, or Dissolved (confirmers only).

Please remember

  • If a yellow banner appears at the top of the site, open this page and give each new person your own Yes, Maybe, or No for their group.
  • When you use Add someone below, enter their name and job title only — do not put in email addresses or other personal details.
  • This list helps us compare views; the Project Manager / Project Owner still confirms who attends meetings.
