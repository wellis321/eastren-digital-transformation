Why This Programme Exists

Two problems, one plan.

Housing Repairs has an imperative to replace its store and job costing system, Servitor, which becomes unsupported in early 2027. Separately, there's a long-standing need to give trade operatives real mobile working capability — the information they need to plan and deliver a repair, in their hands, without paper and without re-keying it back at the office.

Rather than treat these as two projects, the programme delivers the mobile repairs transformation as part of the plan to retire Servitor. One procurement, one platform, one connected process — not a second system bolted on afterwards.

Our Vision

A best-in-class repair service, not a like-for-like swap.

Deliver a best-in-class repair service solution — selected from proven technology already used widely in local government and housing associations — covering the whole job from end to end:

Capture Plan Schedule Stock Report Pay

Where necessary, the solution fully integrates with existing systems to keep that a genuinely robust, end-to-end process — with the least possible manual intervention required at any step.

Improved Customer Experience

The reason the programme exists. Eight benefits deliver it, each tied to a real gap in how the service runs today.

Improved customer experience is the reason for all of it: repairs done right first time, appointments kept, homes ready sooner, and tenants told what's happening with their repair instead of having to chase it by phone. The eight benefits below are how we get there.

Stock Control

Stores and vans brought under proper control, replacing today's largely paper-based stock request, replenishment and return processes. For tenants: operatives have the part and fix it first time.

Lower Emissions

Dynamic, intelligence-led scheduling and better working practices cut unnecessary travel between jobs. For tenants: appointments that are tighter and easier to keep.

Less Paper, Fewer Delays

Reducing paper-based processes within repairs so the service relies less on manual re-entry, and less on people being available to do it. For tenants: jobs move on without waiting on paperwork.

Better Business Intelligence

Real-time KPI visibility and escalation, and reporting that reflects what's actually happening in the service right now. For tenants: problems spotted before tenants have to chase us.

Financial Control

Tighter, more accurate financial control and monitoring, from job costing through to invoicing. For tenants: tenants' rent goes further.

Safer Working

Repairs operatives given real improvements to their safety on every job, building on the lone working system already in place — not an afterthought, but a named goal of the programme. For tenants: safe, well-supported operatives in their homes.

Customer Communication

Improved customer communication and satisfaction — tenants told what's happening with their repair, not left to chase it by phone. For tenants: they always know where their repair stands.

Void Performance

Better void property performance — turning empty homes around faster, with proper visibility of where each one stands. For tenants: new tenants get their keys sooner.

Delivered in Three Phases

Several elements run in parallel, but the priority sequence runs from procurement now through to full replacement by April 2027.

Phase 1 — from April 2025
Procure & Replace the Core

Procurement of a mobile working solution for repairs delivery, alongside replacement of Servitor itself (Civica's stock and job costing solution) and integration with Integra for greater financial control and monitoring.

Underway
Phase 2
Extend Mobile Working & Core Integrations

Integration with NEC Housing for repairs reporting and monitoring; introducing mobile working capability for inspections, void properties and reactive repairs; the potential replacement of the Dynamic Resource Scheduler (DRS); and deployment and management of suitable handheld devices.

Planned
Phase 3 — through to April 2027
Complete the Landscape

Replacement of Datastox (barcoding and stock allocation); integration with iTrent for people management; a review of the Repairs Delivery service structure and the Business Support Team's functionality and roles; and support for the wider stores management strategic plan.

Planned

Where We Are Now

These goals sit inside a live, moving programme — here's what's genuinely happened so far.

  • ROCC selected as the repairs and works management solution, replacing Servitor and DRS
  • System walkthroughs completed across NECH, DRS, Integra and APEX, alongside ROCC itself
  • Cross-council learning session held with North Ayrshire Council, an established ROCC user
  • Project Initiation Document (PID) in development with ROCC ahead of the first delivery workshops
  • Governance fully stood up — see the full governance structure
  • The Get Involved site is live, with 97 tenants already volunteering to help shape the new service
