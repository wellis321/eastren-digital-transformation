Voids — Resources

Resources & Further Reading

The source material behind the Voids pages — sector benchmarking data, regulatory guidance, parliamentary papers, and the actual landlord policies referenced throughout. Useful for anyone on the team who wants to go past the summary and read the primary source.

External links

These all open in a new tab and take you to third-party sites — Scotland’s Housing Network, the Scottish Parliament, the Scottish Housing Regulator, and individual RSLs. A couple are PDFs that will download directly.

Sector Data & Benchmarking

The numbers behind the case for treating void turnaround as a priority.

SHN Charter Insights 2024/25 — Public Report
Report
Scotland’s Housing Network’s public report on performance against the Scottish Social Housing Charter, drawn from its member benchmarking data — includes average re-let times and rental income lost to void properties across participating landlords.
Visit Resource ↗
Housemark Research: £1.6bn in Repairs, Lettings & Lost Rent
Research
Coverage of Housemark research (via the Northern Ireland Housing Executive) quantifying the UK-wide cost of void properties — combining lost rent, repair costs, and letting costs into a single sector-wide figure.
Visit Resource ↗

Policy, Regulation & Parliament

The statutory and regulatory backdrop, plus the current parliamentary conversation around void turnaround and the housing emergency.

Scottish Housing Regulator — Regulatory Framework
Regulatory
The SHR’s official regulatory framework for landlords — sets out how performance against the Scottish Social Housing Charter is assessed, including void and re-let metrics, and what happens when a landlord falls short.
Visit Resource ↗
Scottish Parliament — Local Government, Housing & Planning Committee Paper
Parliamentary
A 2025 committee paper covering housing performance issues in Scotland, including the void and re-let delays caused by utility company bottlenecks — the source for the Minister for Housing’s comments on this as a “particular challenge”.
Visit Resource ↗
SPICe Issues Paper
Parliamentary Briefing
A Scottish Parliament Information Centre (SPICe) briefing paper providing independent research and analysis to support parliamentary scrutiny of housing policy.
Visit Resource ↗
Audit Scotland — Low Demand Properties
Report (Archive)
An Audit Scotland report examining low-demand social housing stock — properties that are repeatedly refused or attract little to no waiting list. Note the file path indicates this is a 2004 report, so treat it as historical background on the problem rather than current data.
Visit Resource ↗
CIH: Delivering Scotland's Affordable Homes
Report
A Chartered Institute of Housing (CIH) report on delivering affordable homes in Scotland, set against the backdrop of the declared housing emergency and the pressure it places on existing stock — including the trade-offs landlords face between new supply and managing voids in existing stock.
Visit Resource ↗

Landlord Policies & Practice

The actual working policies referenced on What Others Are Doing, plus a recorded session on building a void management strategy.

Maryhill Housing — Repairs & Maintenance Policy
Policy Document
Maryhill Housing’s published repairs and maintenance policy — covers reactive repair categorisation (Emergency/Urgent/Routine), void categorisation into 3/5/10/12-day bands, and the Voids Incentive Scheme.
Visit Resource ↗
ng homes — Void Management Policy
Policy Document
ng homes’ published void management policy — covers the 25 working day re-let target and the Tenants Rewards Scheme (£50 for following the correct end-of-tenancy procedure).
Visit Resource ↗
CIH: Developing an Effective Void Management Strategy
Recording
A recorded CIH session on building an effective void management strategy — practitioner-level discussion rather than a written policy document.
