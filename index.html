Housing Repairs Digital Transformation

A working portal for ERC's Housing Repairs Digital Transformation programme — tracking delivery progress, mapping sector context, and bringing together the policy and best-practice foundations that underpin the work.

Commenced: April 2026
Role: Project Manager (Housing Digital Transformation) — William Ellis
Organisation: East Renfrewshire Council
Programme: MAP — Modern, Ambitious Programme

The Programme

ERC's Housing service is leading a digital transformation programme to enhance the productivity of the Housing Repairs service and improve the experience for our tenants. This work spans a 20-month delivery window, working with a range of internal and external stakeholders across repairs, stores, finance, customer services, and digital teams.

About This Portal

This portal brings together ERC’s published documents, Scottish Government strategy, sector benchmarking, and digital delivery best practice into a single navigable resource for the programme team. It sits alongside the live working documents in SharePoint and is intended as a reference and communication tool.
