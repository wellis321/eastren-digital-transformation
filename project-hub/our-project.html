Project Background

Housing Repairs must replace Servitor (Civica's stock and job costing system), which becomes unsupported in early 2027. There is also a long-standing need to give trade operatives real mobile working: the information they need to plan and deliver a repair, in their hands, without paper and without re-keying it at the office. The programme treats these as one initiative: one procurement, one platform (ROCC, selected to replace Servitor and DRS) and one connected process from capture to plan, schedule, stock, report and pay, integrated with NEC, Integra, APEX, iTrent and DATAstox with the least possible manual intervention. Improved customer experience is the driver: repairs right first time, appointments kept, homes re-let sooner, and tenants told what is happening with their repair.

In scope

  • Mobile devices and sign-in for trades
  • Voids brought into the same record (Orchestrator)

Out of scope, or changed

  • DATAstox stays. ROCC doesn't provide the barcoding we need, so we link the two rather than replace it.
  • The fleet system is a separate project. We design alongside it so operatives do one vehicle check, not two.
  • NEC Housing isn't being replaced.

Put this in each workshop's checklist

Planner tasks have a checklist. Use the six asks from the Workshop Outcomes deck, plus that workshop's own “sign it worked” check:

  1. Concerns raised in the room If no objection is raised, agreement is assumed. Anything needing board approval leaves with an owner and a date.
  2. Every data template has a named ERC owner and source system
  3. Decisions circulated and RAID log updated within a week
  4. Today's process captured, not just the future one
  5. Unmet objectives listed, with a follow-on session booked
  6. Attendees can explain the decisions to their team by their next team meeting
