ROCC Data Sheets

The desktop app the teams use to work through ROCC's data templates: what each sheet and column needs, the terms and codes to agree first, and a place to fill sheets in and export them back to Excel. Three short video lessons are built in.

On the app's Learning page, with subtitles and chapters you can jump between. Watch them in order: each one builds on the last.

The three takeawaysAgree each term and code once, first, because renaming a code after loading breaks every row that points at it. Fill the sheets in band order: terms, then Finance and Property, then Repairs and Jobs, then Scheduling, mobile and delivery. For any sheet, ask: data, decision or both? Ours to type, or coming through an interface? Where does it come from and who owns it? Which mandatory columns first?

Where your entries are savedThe app saves what you type in this browser only. Entries made in the copy on your Mac won't appear here, and the other way round. Export to Excel to share or keep a record. Don't type tenant or employee personal details into it.
