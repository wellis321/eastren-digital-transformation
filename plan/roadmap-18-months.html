Roadmap

18‑month delivery roadmap (draft skeleton)

This page is intentionally structured for continuous refinement. It captures what we will deliver, how we will govern it, and what we will measure — while keeping assumptions and unknowns explicit.

What I’ll do next

I’ll pull in your existing 20‑month phased plan from the interview portal and reshape it into an 18‑month in‑role plan with clear outcomes, dependencies (data/integration/procurement), and “decision points”.

Roadmap  Dependencies  Measures

Phase view (placeholder headings)

The detailed content will be filled from ERC context + your existing work. For now this is the “shape” of the plan.

0–6 weeks

Mobilise & baseline

  • Confirm outcomes, scope boundaries, and constraints.
  • Baseline service performance and pain points.
  • Stakeholder map + governance cadence.

Months 2–4

Discovery & options

  • End-to-end journey mapping (tenant + staff).
  • Data/integration constraints mapped.
  • Shortlist options with costs/risks.

Months 4–9

Build & pilot

  • Pilot new processes and digital journeys.
  • Training + adoption plan.
  • Measure and iterate.

Months 9–14

Scale & embed

  • Roll-out to all areas / cohorts.
  • Operational runbooks + support model.
  • Automation and reporting enhancements.

Months 14–18

Benefits & BAU

  • Benefits realisation evidence.
  • BAU ownership and governance handover.
  • Backlog for year 2+.

Throughout

Quality & assurance

  • Info governance + security checkpoints.
  • Accessibility and inclusion.
  • Supplier / contract management.
