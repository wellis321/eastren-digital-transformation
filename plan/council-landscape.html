Council landscape map

A structured way to track what nearby councils and wider local government are doing. This helps with benchmarking, de-risking supplier decisions, and spotting reusable patterns.

How the map works

  • Strategy — what they say they’re trying to do.
  • Capabilities — customer/CRM, case management, repairs, data platform, integration, identity.
  • Delivery model — how they organise product teams, governance, and suppliers.
  • Signals — what’s actually shipping (news posts, service changes, open procurement).
